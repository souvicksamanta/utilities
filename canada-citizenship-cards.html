Discover Canada Quick Cards

The most-tested facts from Discover Canada, the official citizenship study guide, on short cards. Read them in Study mode first. Then switch to Quiz mode, try each card in your head, and tap to check. Press ★ on the cards you know.

Source: Discover Canada: The Rights and Responsibilities of Citizenship, Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada. The test can also ask about current office-holders, so check the names in the “Look these up” box on canada.ca before your test.
