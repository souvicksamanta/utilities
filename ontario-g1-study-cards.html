The test & graduated licensing

What the G1 test looks like and the rules you must follow at each level.

📝G1 knowledge test

  • Minimum age: 16
  • Vision test + written knowledge test (bring ID showing legal name, birth date, signature)
  • 40 multiple-choice questions in two parts: 20 rules of the road + 20 road signs
  • Must get 16 / 20 (80%) in each part. Passing one part doesn't save the other.
  • Whole graduated system must be finished within 5 years

🔰G1 conditions (Level One)

  • Blood alcohol: zero (no cannabis/drugs either)
  • Accompanying driver in the front passenger seat: full Class G or higher, at least 4 years of driving experience, BAC under 0.05%
  • Only the accompanying driver may sit in the front seat
  • Every passenger needs a working seatbelt
  • No driving midnight – 5 a.m.
  • No 400-series highways posted over 80 km/h, and no QEW, Don Valley Pkwy, Gardiner Expwy, E.C. Row Expwy or Conestoga Pkwy. Exception: the accompanying driver is a licensed driving instructor.

🗓️Timeline

  • G1 lasts 12 months, or 8 months with an approved Beginner Driver Education course (BDE = 40 hrs: 20 class + 10 in-car + 10 flexible)
  • Pass the G1 road test to get G2
  • G2 lasts at least 12 months, then the G road test (includes highway driving)
  • Whole process: at least 20 months

🚙G2 conditions (Level Two)

  • BAC zero; seatbelt for every passenger
  • Can drive alone, any road, any time
  • Drivers aged 19 and under, midnight to 5 a.m.:
    first 6 months: only 1 passenger aged 19 or under
    after 6 months (until full G or age 20): up to 3 passengers aged 19 or under
  • Exempt: a fully licensed driver in the front seat, or passengers who are immediate family

🪪Licence paperwork

  • Carry your licence whenever you drive; show it when police ask
  • Report a change of name or address within 6 days
  • Car must have a valid vehicle permit (ownership) and liability insurance. Carry the insurance card.
  • Drivers 21 and under (any licence class) must have zero BAC

👶Child car seats

  • Under 9 kg (20 lb): rear-facing seat
  • 9–18 kg (20–40 lb): forward-facing seat with tether
  • 18–36 kg (40–80 lb), under 8 years and under 145 cm: booster seat
  • Seatbelt alone once the child is 8 years old, or 36 kg+, or 145 cm+
  • Safest spot for kids under 13: the back seat
  • The driver is responsible for passengers under 16 buckling up

Rules of the road

Right-of-way, special vehicles, turns, passing, parking and emergencies.

🔀Right-of-way

  • All-way stop: first vehicle to stop goes first. If two stop at the same time, yield to the vehicle on your right.
  • Uncontrolled intersection (no signs/lights): also yield to the vehicle on the right.
  • Left turn: yield to oncoming traffic and pedestrians.
  • Yield sign: slow down or stop and give way to traffic and pedestrians.
  • Entering a road from a private road or driveway: yield to vehicles on the road and to pedestrians.
  • Right-of-way is something you give, not take. Never insist on it if it would cause a collision.

✋Where to stop

  • At the stop line. No line? Stop at the crosswalk. No crosswalk? Stop at the edge of the sidewalk. No sidewalk? Stop at the edge of the intersection.
  • Come to a complete stop. Rolling stops are illegal.

🚶Pedestrians

  • Pedestrian crossover (PXO) or school crossing guard: stop and wait until the pedestrian or guard has completely crossed the road.
  • Never pass another vehicle within 30 m of a PXO.
  • A pedestrian with a white cane or guide dog always gets the right-of-way.

🚌School buses

  • Amber overhead lights flashing: slow down and prepare to stop.
  • Red lights flashing or stop arm out: stop from both directions.
  • Behind the bus: stop at least 20 m back.
  • Road with a median: only traffic behind the bus must stop.
  • Wait until the bus moves or the lights stop flashing. Fine + 6 points.

🚑Emergency vehicles & Move Over

  • Siren or flashing red/blue lights behind you: pull to the right edge and stop. Stay clear of intersections.
  • On a one-way road, pull to the nearest edge.
  • Stopped emergency vehicle or tow truck with lights flashing: slow down and move over a lane if safe. (3 points)
  • Don't follow a fire truck within 150 m.
  • Snowplows and road maintenance vehicles use flashing blue lights. Stay back; don't pass.

🚋Streetcars

  • Pass on the right only (unless on a one-way street).
  • Stopped with doors open: stop 2 m behind the rear doors and wait for passengers to clear.

🚂Railway crossings

  • Train coming, or lights/gate down: stop at least 5 m from the nearest rail or gate.
  • Never drive around, under or through a gate (3 points).
  • Don't change gears while crossing the tracks.
  • Stalled on the tracks: get everyone out and well away, then call for help.
  • Buses stop at unprotected crossings; school buses stop at all crossings.

↪️Turns

  • Signal well ahead. Right turn: keep close to the right curb, end in the right lane.
  • Left turn: from the left-most lane into the left-most lane of the new road.
  • Right on red is OK after a full stop, unless a sign prohibits it.
  • Left on red is OK only from a one-way road onto a one-way road, after a full stop.
  • U-turns are not allowed on a curve or hill, or near a railway crossing, bridge or tunnel where you can't see 150 m both ways.

⏩Passing

  • Pass on the left. Return only when you can see the passed vehicle's front in your inside mirror.
  • Passing on the right is allowed on multi-lane roads, or around a vehicle turning left when there's a clear lane. Never on the shoulder.
  • No passing: solid line on your side, hills, curves, near intersections, railway crossings, or within 30 m of a PXO.
  • Being passed? Keep right and don't speed up.

🛣️Highways & freeways

  • Entering: use the acceleration lane to match traffic speed, signal, merge into a gap.
  • Exiting: signal early and slow down in the deceleration lane, not on the freeway.
  • Missed your exit? Go on to the next one. Never back up on a freeway.
  • Keep right except to pass. Slower traffic keeps right.
  • HOV lanes (diamond ◇): only vehicles with the posted number of occupants.

🔄Roundabouts

  • Travel counter-clockwise. Yield to traffic already in the roundabout (coming from your left).
  • Never stop inside the roundabout. Signal right before your exit.
  • Emergency vehicle coming? Exit first, then pull over.
  • Pedestrians: yield at the crosswalks before entering and after exiting.

🚲Sharing the road

  • Cyclists: leave at least 1 m when passing. Check your blind spot before opening a door ("dooring" a cyclist = fine + points).
  • Large trucks have big blind spots ("no-zones"). If you can't see their mirrors, they can't see you. They make wide right turns.
  • Slow-moving vehicle (orange triangle with red border): max speed about 40 km/h. Be patient.
  • Motorcycles get a full lane width.

⛰️Parking on a hill

  • Downhill (with or without a curb): wheels turned toward the curb/right.
  • Uphill with a curb: wheels turned away from the curb (left), roll back to touch it.
  • Uphill, no curb: wheels turned right.
  • Always set the parking brake; leave it in Park (or in gear for a manual).

🅿️Where you can't park

  • Within 3 m of a fire hydrant
  • Within 9 m of an intersection, or 15 m if it has lights
  • Within 15 m of a level railway crossing
  • On or within 100 m of a bridge
  • Within 6 m of a public entrance (hotel, theatre, hall)
  • On a curve or hill, or anywhere you can't be seen from 125 m
  • Blocking a sidewalk, crosswalk, driveway or another vehicle

🌧️Night & bad weather

  • Fog: low beams. High beams reflect back and blind you.
  • Rain: roads are slipperiest in the first few minutes. Watch for hydroplaning. If it happens, ease off the gas; don't brake hard.
  • Black ice is most likely on bridges, overpasses and in shaded spots.
  • Daytime running lights are not headlights. They don't light your tail lights.
  • Blinded by oncoming high beams: look toward the right edge of the road.

🆘Emergencies

  • Skid: ease off the gas and brake; steer where you want to go.
  • ABS brakes: press hard and hold; don't pump. No ABS: threshold brake or pump.
  • Brake failure: pump the pedal, shift to a lower gear, apply the parking brake gradually.
  • Stuck gas pedal: shift to neutral, brake, pull off the road.
  • Blowout: grip the wheel firmly, ease off the gas, don't brake hard.
  • Wheels drop off the pavement: grip the wheel, ease off the gas, steer back gently once slowed.
  • Car going into water: undo your seatbelt, open the window, get out.

💥Collisions

  • Stay at the scene. Leaving is 7 points and a criminal offence.
  • Call police if anyone is hurt, or damage is over $5,000.
  • Otherwise exchange information (name, address, licence, plate, insurance) with the other driver.
  • Turn on hazard lights; set up warning signs or flares if safe.

🤚Hand signals

  • Left turn: left arm straight out
  • Right turn: left arm bent up at the elbow (or right arm straight out)
  • Stop / slow: left arm bent down at the elbow

Traffic lights, signals & pavement markings

What each light means, plus pedestrian signals and lines on the road.

🚸Pedestrian signals

  • Walking figure: pedestrians may cross.
  • Flashing or solid orange hand: don't start crossing. Those already crossing should finish.
  • Countdown: seconds left to finish crossing.
  • Intersection pedestrian signal (IPS): a pedestrian push-button signal on a main road; side streets have stop signs.

🔌Lights not working

  • Treat the intersection as an all-way stop.
  • A police officer directing traffic overrides all signals and signs.

〰️Pavement markings

  • Yellow lines separate traffic going in opposite directions.
  • White lines separate lanes going the same direction.
  • Solid line = don't cross or pass. Broken line = you may pass when safe.
  • Solid + broken: you may pass only if the broken line is on your side.
  • Double solid yellow: no passing either way.
  • Two-way left-turn lane: middle lane edged with solid + broken yellow on both sides. Use it only to turn left, from either direction.
  • Diamond (◇): reserved lane (HOV, bus, taxi or bicycle).
  • Stop line (thick white across lane), crosswalk lines, turn arrows.
  • Yield line ("shark teeth" ▽▽▽): yield here, e.g. at roundabouts.

Road signs

Learn the shape and colour first. They tell you the sign type before you read it.

Octagon: STOP only
Triangle: YIELD only
Yellow diamond: warning of a hazard ahead
Orange diamond: temporary condition (construction)
Pentagon (yellow-green): school zone
White rectangle/square: regulatory (law). Green circle = allowed; red circle + slash = not allowed.
Green: direction · Blue: services · Brown: tourism/recreation
X crossbuck: railway crossing (treat it like a yield sign)

Other penalties

Alcohol, drugs, phone use and stunt driving.

🍺Alcohol & drugs

  • G1/G2, M1/M2, and all drivers 21 and under: must be zero alcohol and drugs. Any amount = immediate 24-hour roadside suspension; if convicted, a fine and at least a 30-day suspension.
  • Warn range 0.05–0.08 (fully licensed): 1st = 3-day suspension · 2nd = 7 days + alcohol education program · 3rd = 30 days + treatment + ignition interlock
  • Over 0.08, or refusing a test: immediate 90-day suspension + 7-day vehicle impound + fees
  • Criminal conviction: at least a 1-year suspension; later offences can mean a lifetime ban
  • Refusing a breath, blood or drug test counts the same as failing it

📵Distracted driving (hand-held phone)

  • Fully licensed: fine of $615–$1,000, 3 points, 3-day suspension (1st offence)
  • Novice (G1/G2): no demerit points; instead escalating suspensions: 30 days → 90 days → licence cancelled
  • Hands-free use only. Phones may be handled only when you're safely parked off the road, or to call 911.

🏎️Stunt driving / racing

  • Speeding 40+ km/h over on roads posted under 80, 50+ over on roads posted 80 or more, or 150+ km/h anywhere
  • Immediate roadside 30-day licence suspension + 14-day vehicle impound
  • If convicted: fine of $2,000–$10,000, up to 6 months in jail, a longer suspension, and 6 points

⛔Other ways to lose your licence

  • Driving while suspended: fines in the thousands, up to 6 months in jail, 6 months added to the suspension, 7-day impound
  • Driving without insurance: fine of $5,000–$50,000
  • Medical conditions (doctors must report ones that affect driving)
  • Unpaid court-ordered fines or family support

Memory tricks

Short hooks to lock in the numbers.

  • "Twenty for the bus": stop 20 m behind a school bus. "Five for the train": stop 5 m from the rail.
  • 150 is the magic number: fire truck following distance, dimming for oncoming cars, headlights-on visibility, U-turn sight distance.
  • 60 = following: dim your high beams within 60 m of the car ahead.
  • Hydrant 3, Intersection 9, Lights or Train 15 (parking distances in metres).
  • Novice 2 · 6 · 9 (letter · letter · 60-day suspension) vs Full 6 · 9 · 15 (letter · letter · 30-day).
  • Points ladder 3-4-6-7: 16+ over = 3 · 30+ = 4 · 50+ = 6 · leaving a crash or fleeing police = 7.
  • Shapes first: octagon = stop, triangle = yield, diamond = warning, pentagon = school, X = railway crossing, white rectangle = law you must obey.
  • Colours: Red = stop/forbidden · Yellow = warning · Orange = construction · Green = go/direction · Blue = services · Brown = tourism · Fluorescent yellow-green = school/pedestrians.
  • Yield to the right at a 4-way stop tie or an uncontrolled intersection. Yield to the left when entering a roundabout.
  • Car seats 9 · 18 · 36: rear-facing under 9 kg → forward-facing to 18 kg → booster to 36 kg (or until age 8 / 145 cm).
  • Flashing green = "go, you own the left" (protected left turn). Flashing red = stop sign. Flashing yellow = caution.
